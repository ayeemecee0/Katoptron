Interpreting progress

The secondary sharing-network catalog is far larger than the independently sourced original publisher evidence. That difference is evidence coverage, not a software-completion percentage. A growing number of original document leads is only useful when review converts them into confirmed original-source facts. We report both separately.

Government documents discovered by automated crawlers must be examined for actual dates, document identity, signatories, sharing direction and applicable policies before publication as events. A failed or blocked page is never automatically a revoked sharing arrangement.
