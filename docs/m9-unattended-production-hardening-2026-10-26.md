## Supabase advisor and current limits

- **No anonymous-callable public SECURITY DEFINER routines** after source-authority migration 143.
- **12 authenticated-callable privileged routines** remain for contributor ownership, reviewer moderation, and editorial workflows, subject to owner/reviewer checks and preexisting SQL/API regressions. Any new function grant must be independently audited.
- **46 RLS-enabled private tables with no public policies** are informational: their default-deny posture is expected for service-role/owner-only queues. Do not add permissive policies just to silence the INFO indicator.
- **Leaked-password protection** is not active. Supabase documentation specifies Pro-plan availability, so this cannot be claimed solved under a $0 budget.
- **31 unused indexes** are performance INFO signals, not a mandate to blindly drop indexes; few public submissions exist and the workload is evolving.
- The 500 MB Free database allowance, 1 GB org-wide Storage allowance and absence of managed Free backups must remain explicit. Monitor cannot establish usage in provider systems that do not expose quota metadata through the existing credentials.

## Operational decision

**Do not mark M9 100%** or enable an unrestricted private-contribution production launch merely because CI/browser/security tests are green. Preserve public evidence-first beta operation, actual user private records and encrypted recovery hygiene. Prioritize production-grade sender reputation, owner-controlled backup and real-user accessibility checkpoints. Automation must remain truthful about incomplete provider-account and user acceptance gates.

**M9 preparation estimate: 90% (up from the previous 75% estimate), blocked on externally unverifiable owner/human controls.** This is a planning estimate, not a cryptographic/release assurance score.
