# M10 · Oklahoma City 2026 signed Flock amendment, approved renewal and original council evidence

**Original source-page review:** October 27, 2026. City Council decision / signed amendment dated August 18, 2026.

## Material conclusion: actual original signed amendment, not merely an unsigned proposal

The City of Oklahoma City's [public Flock LPR program page](https://www.okc.gov/Services/Public-Safety/Police/Flock-Safety-license-plate-reader-LPR-usage-in-Oklahoma-City) confirms that on **August 18, 2026, City Council voted to approve a $270,000 renewal** through June 2027, and links to the original government amendment, staff memorandum, renewal and encumbering certificate. This city-authored report is a public government declaration, not a court finding, device telemetry or an independently audited procurement ledger.

The original [2-page Flock Amendment No. 1](https://www.okc.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/police/documents/flock/flock-amendment-august-2026.pdf) has been directly retrieved and individually visually inspected:

- **Original page 1** inserts Section 4.4 into the 2023 agreement, prohibiting Flock from **voluntarily providing city data to federal agencies**, including through federated search tools, hotlists, databases, Flock-affiliated entities and intermediaries. The City retains its own contractual ability to share its City data. The clause contemplates compulsory legal process, notice where lawful, legal assistance and minimum legally required disclosure. This is **not** an absolute ban on all federal access, a technical access audit or proof the live system implements the setting.
- **Original page 2** visibly includes **APPROVED by Council and signed by the Mayor ... 18TH ... AUGUST ... 2026**, city clerk attestation and city seal. A counterpart Flock Group legal-officer signature is dated **July 24, 2026**. The amendment's opening says it becomes effective upon the last signature. This establishes an **executed original agreement** with dated signatures, rather than a draft or unapproved staff document.

The original [21-page City Finance Department Renewal No. 3 package](https://www.okc.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/police/documents/flock/flock-renewal-letter-august-2026.pdf) was SHA-pinned as a whole, but **only original page 1 has been individually visually reviewed**; the other pages are supporting insurance documents and are not claimed as source-page-reviewed contract obligations. Page 1 bears an **August 18, 2026 approved-by-city-council stamp** and Flock's vendor-concurrence signature. It specifies contract **C241032**, for **July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027**. The letter explicitly says vendor concurrence alone does not guarantee renewal, which is why the City's separate approval stamp and city publication matter.

The [2-page City Manager's Council memorandum](https://www.okc.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/police/documents/flock/flock-council-memo-august-2026.pdf) requests an amendment and renewal, retroactive to July 1, 2026, with a **$270,000** proposed cost. It describes the 2023 master agreement and 2024–2025 renewal sequence, and names the Police Sales Tax Fund as the source of funds. **The memo is a staff recommendation; it is not independently the August 18 Council vote record.**

The [1-page city availability-of-funds certificate](https://www.okc.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/police/documents/flock/availability-of-funds-certificate-august-2026.pdf) is visibly signed by the encumbering officer and certifies **$270,000 in sufficient available balance**. **Financial authorization is not the same as a paid invoice, actual disbursement or verified entire contract spend.**

## Original city program page, explicitly city-authored and source dated

Oklahoma City's program publication, as accessed October 27, 2026, reports:

- **90 city-owned Flock readers as of 2026**. This is the City's self-reported inventory, **not independently verified camera point positions**, equipment telemetry, or an authorization to place 90 unverified map pins.
- **109 law enforcement partners granted data access as of August 18, 2026**. This is a **single dated aggregate City statement**, not 109 individually identified, corroborated sharing-network edges or continuous current permissions.
- **7-day ordinary data retention**, with case-specific retention beyond seven days described as an exception. This is a city description of its policy/configuration, **not an independent audit of actual enforcement or permanent technical settings**.
- **August 18, 2026 City Council approval of the $270,000 contract renewal through June 2027**, supported by the signed/stamped government original documents. This does not prove expenditure.
