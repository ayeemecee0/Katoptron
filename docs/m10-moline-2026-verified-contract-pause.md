**Source review: October 27, 2026.**

## What the original government documents actually establish

1. **Original signed Flock order, December 2024:** The city-hosted six-page scan has 2024-12-03 and 2024-12-04 signatures. It records a four-Falcon-reader order, an 11-month initial term, 24-month renewal provisions, 30-day data retention, and stated order pricing. This is a historic order, not an active camera inventory. It remains independently SHA-pinned in the existing nine-document baseline.
2. **Signed contractual pause, September 2026:** [Moline's published one-page signed Flock pause agreement](https://moline.il.us/DocumentCenter/View/16700/Flock-Safety-Contract-Pause-September-2026) is a raster scan, with signatures visibly dated September 14 (city) and September 15 (Flock). It requires Flock to remove fixed readers, terminate the city's platform access, and pause the contracts for a **one-time $4,500 fee**. The agreement calls for removal within 30 days; the permitted poles may stay until the end of the contract. Conditional future provisions: service resumption before February 1, 2028 restarts prorated billing; installation of another fixed ALPR vendor before then could trigger payment of amounts otherwise due under original contracts; if there is no such installation, the Flock contracts terminate without further payment obligation arising from the pause/new vendor decision after that date. These are **contractual commitments and conditions**, not confirmation they were carried out.
3. **City announcement, September 15, 2026:** [Moline Police ALPR transparency page](https://www.moline.il.us/1955/License-Plate-Readers) describes a nine-camera program and an expectation that all fixed cameras would be deactivated in roughly 14 days. Physical removal was expected to follow later. The page does **not** certify actual deactivation, removal, current operation, current billing, or equipment locations.

**Do not infer:** That four 2024 contracted readers grew by exactly five units; that nine readers are currently operating; that the announced deactivation occurred on September 29; that the order was fully paid; or that the fixed-camera pause terminated all future rights and obligations immediately.
