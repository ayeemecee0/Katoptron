# M10 · Green Bay originals in Agency X-Ray

## Document selection

This release does not acquire new original source files. It promotes four **already independently source-page-reviewed** Green Bay Police Department government originals from the strict national manifest into the agency-specific X-Ray and comparison indexes. No Flock camera position or active sharing edge is inferred.

| Original | Source date | Evidence role | Original pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Department September 2026 ALPR monthly report | Compiled October 1, 2026 | Self-reported historical activity and named access recipients, not a confirmed live network | 1 |
| Policy 424 published May 2026 | May 4, 2026 | Version-specific written 30-day ordinary retention language, no proof currently in force or enforced | 2 |
| Proposed Policy 424 revision marked DRAFT | **Unverified effective/adoption date**; document copyright September 13, 2026 | Proposal for seven-day ordinary retention and further restrictions/audits; **not adopted** | 2, 4, 6 |
| Government-hosted 2025-labeled Flock vendor order form | **Execution/effective date unverified** | Original form lists equipment quantities, 30-day retention language and five-year annual pricing, not confirmed paid/installed hardware | 2, 4 |

The published original record also describes four recipients granted access in the October 1 aggregate report. This is a publisher statement at one historical date, not a verified reciprocal sharing edge, current access configuration, individual plate read or live search.

## Temporal integrity

The four sources **must not** be converted to a single current-policy setting, $1,392,000 actually spent, 42 known installed camera positions, a change from 30 to seven days that is legally in force, or confirmed compliance. Neither the 2025 vendor form nor the September draft gets an invented date; the UI displays the precise original date qualification. The dated May published version cannot be presumed still effective as of today.

## User value

The existing topic-filtered Agency X-Ray comparison now shows both Green Bay's 30-day published written policy and its draft seven-day proposal, with page-specific citations and dates. The associated original order terms and historical activity report remain visible in the all-topics view. This changes the index from 17 agencies / 21 verified originals to **18 agencies / 25 originals**, subject to passing source-bound validators and production acceptance.
