# Katoptron pilot relevance: rollout and long-term intelligence milestones

## Delivered in this sprint
- A no-login starting research workflow supports five non-identifying professional beats: local accountability, investigative technology, civil liberties, legal/records, and public spending.
- Only original-government records already checked by `KatoptronGovernmentDocket.build` enter the recommendations. Recommendations expose historical status, original URLs, publisher evidence and unresolved questions.
- Matching is by topic, public jurisdiction and optional question, with reproducible rank criteria. Scores represent **relevance to the research task**, not truth, urgency, corruption likelihood or risk.
- Links may include **only** a preset ID and jurisdiction key. They never contain reporter name, email, private questions or follow lists. Selection happens client-side, with no analytics ingestion or change to RLS.
- Complete downloadable HTML packets include all reviewed original-government records for the jurisdiction, sorted within the source-selection logic and each with date/status/source/proof. Incomplete coverage is disclosed; no new evidence fabricated.
- Two additional original sources added to the historical-only docket, for **Houston, TX** (September 28, 2022 Flock maximum-ceiling amendment agenda, item 40, verified PDF page 7) and **Louisville, KY** (May 6, 2026 district fund report item O-094-26 for proposed $6,650 lease/installation). Neither demonstrates actual payments, signed amendments or active equipment.

## Pilot usage
The 19 outbound recipients are not published in the repository or linked to a specific jurisdiction without explicit choice. For a pilot follow-up, invite each recipient to select their exact locality and beat using `pilot-research.html`. Ask whether the selected package was useful or novel; make no unverified claims of personalization to an identified reporter.

## Next milestones, in value order
1. **P0 – Outreach pilot usage:** 5 researchers open their beat/jurisdiction public source bundle, 3 complete one independent task; record novelty, time saved and useful/incorrect claims without personal surveillance data.
2. **P0 – Original-government acquisition:** verify 25 additional *distinct* original records in underserved relevant jurisdictions. Each record must pass issuer identity, actual date/status and original URL verification; unknown and held candidates are not counted.
3. **P0 – Relevance feedback loop:** voluntary lead usefulness/novelty flag and opt-in retention measured for 10 pilot researchers; avoid personal research disclosure.
4. **P1 – Contract and policy effective-date comparisons:** 10 reviewer-approved, original-document page-cited before/after changes and differences in terms, not inferred wrongdoing.
5. **P1 – New verified personal intelligence:** publish only new reviewed, original-government changes after opt-in; 10 eligible alerts, zero duplicate sends and zero false retroactive "new" labels.
6. **P1 – Professional tasks:** 10 active researchers and three credible purchase commitments; paid billing remains disabled until a paid-plan hypothesis is validated.
7. **P2 – Team collaboration:** five multi-person investigations using owner-approved access, consented invitations, role-based permissions and audit.
8. **P3 – Nationwide acquisition reliability:** expand original-source publisher registry, automate freshness and broken-link audit in all 50 states, with explicit gaps and revocations.
