## Separately dated 2026 city statements

The 2026 city FAQ says two Flock ALPR cameras in August 2026; it describes license plate *photographs* retained for two years and *Flock/hotlist hits* for 30 days. The city's statements about audits and sharing configurations are its own publication and are not independently inspected current backend settings.

The older auditor's Flock Vehicle Reads setting and newer city's photograph and hit categories cannot automatically be equated. Distinct record types and observation dates prevent a substantiated finding of a retention contradiction or compliance violation.

The city names Department Policy 427. Its final applicable policy text is still separately held for independent original page and version comparison, and this review does not silently upgrade the linked policy manual to final adopted status.
