## Release stance
Maintain **public evidence-limited beta**. Do not advertise nationwide exhaustive coverage or current camera uptime, and never count hypothetical simulations as real plate detections. Preserve original source provenance and consent. No camera count or milestone completion percentage may increase based solely on a private lead, a green workflow, OSM vicinity, or vendor-reported mixed-camera total.

**Independent evidence acquisition**, not code regression testing, is the principal remaining blocker to adding new source-verified government camera points. Owner-operated account settings and real-world human testing are the distinct production-release blockers.
