## Crucial definitions

- **Enrolled:** State research queue and query templates exist; no source or camera claim implied.
- **Government source seeded:** The registry has a known original government publication or source link. This does *not* mean all government records or ALPR agencies in the state have been identified. Some sources are old, non-Flock technologies, statutes, policy templates, proposed bills or procurement **proposals**.
- **Retrieved:** An exact government source returned parseable HTML or PDF, bounded by size/page guards and fingerprinted. A hash or keyword match is not content/agency deployment verification.
- **Held:** The original URL did not pass strict retrieval. Its content is not falsely reported as verified.
- **Individually verified:** A separate original-byte, page-specific, source-dated finding with explicit publisher class. The existing narrower original reports and Washington verified documents retain their independently reviewed status, but the 43 new national source receipts are *not* upgraded into findings by this workflow.
- **Camera coverage:** Requires original equipment source evidence or verified submissions with appropriately labeled precise vs approximate locations. No additional camera coordinates are created here.

## Operational safeguards and next bottlenecks

The [nationwide original-source watcher](../scripts/watch_nationwide_government_originals.py) uses exact HTTPS government publications, an explicit exception for original West Virginia state `www.state.wv.us`, deny-redirect handling, per-response byte and page caps, immutable source hashes for accessible originals, and status receipts for held sources. Its [regression suite](../scripts/test_watch_nationwide_government_originals.py) guards all 51 jurisdictions and rejects forged non-government source domains or camera facts.

The public [national original-source evidence library](../national-audits.html) now contains a searchable 51-jurisdiction publisher index, direct original URL links and separate monitored/accessible/held counts, with no claim of verified nationwide Flock hardware.

**Remaining work is substantial:** original-page verification on the retrieved government documents, alternate original sources for twelve held URLs, a source lead discovery provider yielding useful results (currently zero), agency-by-agency nationwide inventory and camera-map verification, and recurring policy/contract/sharing timeline updates. *All 51 state research queues are implemented, but nationwide Flock evidence depth and camera coverage are not complete.*

Publisher-specific distinctions worth preserving: Vermont and New Hampshire statutory sources are not camera inventories; the Wyoming original is an unadopted bill; New York is a model policy not agency adoption; Nevada is a grant application not installed devices; West Virginia and Maryland include other-vendor ALPR sources, which do not authenticate Flock; the dated Ohio original concerns a historical subscription procurement rather than a live camera network.
