# M10 original-source review completion and multistate expansion
**Verification date:** October 27, 2026. All counts refer to source documents and this bounded source inventory, not active camera installations.

## Minnesota, all 21 prior exact-agency candidates resolved
Starting from 22 externally audited page-reviewed Minnesota originals, all 21 priority historical candidate records were individually inspected against the original [Minnesota Legislative Reference Library](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/mndocs/mandates_detail?orderid=8735) archive. Every original has a source-specific disposition:
- **20 external independent reports** now include at least one page-specific source-attributed historical finding in `data/verified-independent-audits.json`. Those span earlier BOSS/PAGIS-era mobile ALPR snapshots, 2017–2022 Elsag patrol-camera systems, and distinct Axon mobile ALPR source reports in agencies that also have independently reviewed Flock-system reports. Exact PDF SHA-256 hashes and page anchors are verified by `scripts/verify_independent_audit_source.py`. The resulting curated Minnesota external-independent catalog contains **42** originals.
- **1 agency-internal government ALPR audit**, [Hennepin County 2017](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/docs/2019/mandated/191260.pdf), is separately attributed in `data/verified-agency-published-alpr-audits.json` rather than promoted to external independent status. It reports the historical presence of a plate-data log retained longer than the main ALPR purge setting. It is not evidence of Flock, present conduct, or a legal judgment by Katoptron.
- Original [Woodbury 2019](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/docs/2019/mandated/191307.pdf) required a larger bounded download (2.7MB) and was later page-reviewed with hash `5a09ce707e50685e5d56e4619ea9ee217bb42a3a724c77733be6ff00a9c00607`. [Hennepin County 2020](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/docs/2020/mandated/200290.pdf) used PDF AES encryption but publicly permitted the **empty user password**; reinspection found a public nine-page original, hash `5d0c08601ca312d8ca814d62cfa4b93551d77bf79a3b5190924c5c61b60435aa`. The verifier permits empty-password reading only for that exact SHA-pinned report, not arbitrary protected documents, and never attempts secret credentials.

The unreviewed-exact-agency queue for this original 21-record scope is **0**, not a claim that Minnesota has no other audits beyond the curated set. The original state registry still includes **137** report metadata links and **16** titles naming Flock. Non-Flock titles and earlier BOSS software references are not represented as Flock device locations or current deployments.

### Source distinctions and no false device math
Original auditor findings include earlier mobile reader counts and historically absent fixed readers for Edina, Richfield, Brooklyn Center, Robbinsdale, Hennepin and Woodbury, plus Elsag patrol readers in Shakopee. Others give separate Axon mobile unit observations for West Saint Paul, Robbinsdale, Hennepin County, Champlin and University of Minnesota. **BOSS/PAGIS is back-office software, not by itself a reader hardware manufacturer.** Never add Axon patrol ALPR counts to Flock stationary counts, confuse audit dates with installation dates, interpret a source's unavailable result as a withdrawn original, or infer real-time data-sharing edges.

The CA/WI documents are published by police departments. Their provenance and hash/page assertions are independently checked by Katoptron, but the **audits themselves are NOT independent external reviews**. Two states outside Minnesota are seeded: national discovery remains limited, not a nationwide audit census. No person-level plate reads, GPS data or sensitive private case material is acquired or published.

## Continuous evidence controls
1. `scripts/verify_independent_audit_source.py` periodically verifies 42 original state archive PDFs against pinned source SHA-256 and exact page text.
2. `scripts/verify_agency_published_audits.py` separately verifies the four internal/government agency-authored originals, with fixed official hosts, content hashes and exact page anchors. Both workflows fail closed on unavailable or revised public source.
3. `scripts/scan_mn_historical_queue.py` and the public Minnesota library now reconcile independently reviewed originals against separately reviewed agency-internal originals without promoting them to independent status.
4. National public library `national-audits.html` uses text-only safe DOM construction, strictly approved government domains and source-by-source original PDF page links; no untrusted HTML or live plate data.
5. Browser acceptance checks exact original citations, source-tier labels, special San Bruno date ambiguity, mobile layout and filtered state results.

## Remaining limitation and next acquisition target
Source discovery outside Minnesota currently relies on three explicitly researched original city-hosted audit PDFs. There is not yet a credible equivalent to Minnesota's statewide indexed mandate archive for automatically finding all available 2025–2026 local records. The next development phase should validate jurisdiction-specific public document portals and records retention schedules before introducing wider automated ingestion. Do not present source sampling or missing documentation as national representativeness.
