Washington ALPR agency registrations

A searchable, date-stamped copy of the Washington Attorney General's public list of agencies that submitted ALPR registrations. Each agency entry links to the original policy location that appears in the state register.

Self-certification is not independent verification. The Washington Attorney General states that it has not independently verified the certifications agencies submitted. An entry does not confirm use of Flock Safety, the number or positions of any cameras, current system operation, effective policy compliance, or sharing permissions. Some listings concern parking or university systems rather than police surveillance. Submitted policies are not independently page-verified by Katoptron merely because the state site links them. The entries below are a snapshot, not a continuously live feed.
