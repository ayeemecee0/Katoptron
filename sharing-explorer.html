What a link means

A → B means agency A's publicly aggregated portal sharing list named agency B as a permitted user at the source snapshot date. It does not demonstrate that B searched A, that B reciprocates, or that any present permissions remain active. Missing outbound lists mean UNKNOWN or undisclosed, never zero access.

The full network is derived from EyesOnFlock transparency-portal data and normalized by deflock-data, redistributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes in this view: searchable exact source IDs, inbound relationships computed from original outbound links, locality-based navigation and explicit evidence uncertainties. Its agency coordinates are geography centroids, not ALPR camera positions. An independent government/first-party recheck is still required to elevate an edge to first-party-verified status.
