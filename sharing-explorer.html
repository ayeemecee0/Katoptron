Documented Access & Sharing Explorer

Discover what an agency or a dated transparency-portal analysis says about other organizations being permitted to query its Flock network. This is not a record of plate reads or a complete list of agencies.

Direction matters. An agency sharing its records with another agency does not establish reciprocal access. A listed organization having potential access does not prove it ever conducted a search. The observations below are secondary-source snapshots; they have not yet been independently rechecked against every live Flock portal. A missing list is unknown, not zero. Before adding more directional links

Katoptron must save for each link: which agency owns the data, which named agency is authorized to search it, the original portal or policy URL, the source/snapshot date, and whether the reading is direct or secondary. A policy-based access permission is not the same as an audit log showing a search. Proposed one-click briefs will use only those source-qualified observations.

Reuse and attribution: These six secondary summary observations are a subset of the EyesOnFlock portal dataset, presented through Perfect World's September 2026 source index.
